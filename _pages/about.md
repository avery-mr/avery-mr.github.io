# About

I am a Senior 3D Artist and Technical Artist currently working in architectural visualization and virtual design & construction, with a background spanning film, design, VFX, real-time rendering, and interactive media.

Over the past decade I have worked across photorealistic rendering, animation, VR, compositing, procedural simulation, and visualization pipelines using tools such as 3ds Max, VRay, Unreal Engine, Houdini, TyFlow, After Effects, and Resolve. My professional work has focused heavily on creating high-quality visualizations while also improving production workflows and technical processes.

Recently, I returned to the University of Colorado Boulder to complete a B.S. in Computer Science, where my interests have increasingly shifted toward the technical side of graphics and interactive systems. I am particularly interested in:

- Real-time rendering and shader development
- Technical art pipelines and workflow automation
- Graphics programming and OpenGL
- Procedural systems and simulation
- AI-assisted creative tools and workflows
- Computer vision and spatial computing
- The intersection of visualization, engineering, and machine learning

This site serves both as a development journal and a portfolio of ongoing work. Throughout this course I will be documenting weekly progress, experiments, technical studies, and project milestones as I continue exploring potential paths into graphics programming, technical art, visualization engineering, and AI-driven creative tooling.

## Current Focus Areas

- Building small real-time rendering and OpenGL projects
- Exploring procedural and node-based workflows
- Developing stronger software engineering foundations
- Investigating how AI can augment visualization and design workflows
