## Week 3 Standup
This past week I focused primarily on developing my project proposal and trying to better understand what a semester study of OpenGL and computer graphics might realistically look like.  I was eager and tempted to dive in immediately to some of my source material but I decided to spend a little more time trying to map out and understand the major concepts and what could define milestones throughout the semester.

The most challenging aspect of ths so far has been determining how to organize the curriculum around meaningful deliverables, and based on the professor's feedback I've been thinking about the project as a sequence of increasingly complex graphics demonstrations with each milestone resulting in a visible artifact that demonstrates a specific concept.  I also spent a bit of time researching generally the field of computer graphics and how OpenGL (as well as other graphics APIs) fit into the overall ecosystem.  It has become increasingly clear that of the topics I initially imagined as individual milestones could actually be entire courses on their own, and because of the that I am trying to focus less on mastering every aspect and more on building a solid foundation that can support future learning after CSPB.

Next week I plan to polish out the details of my roadmap and deliverable demos and begin working through introductory OpenGL material.  The first practical hurdle will be setting up a stable OpenGL dev environment and deciding which toolchain I will use, configuring libraries, C++ compilers, etc.
